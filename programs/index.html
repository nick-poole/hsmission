Our programs in Haiti

Water, education, relief, and community.

How does Haitian Sensation Mission help Haiti?Through direct community action in three areas: clean water access, quality education, and disaster recovery — plus community development projects that help villages like Pelerin become self-sufficient. 100% of donations fund these programs.

Water

Clean water initiatives

Safe water for Haitian families — wells and filtration for villages without reliable access.

Education

Educational outreach

Opportunity through learning — school meals, supplies, and support for students in Pelerin.

Relief

Disaster relief

Rebuilding after crisis — emergency aid and long-term recovery support.

Community outreach

Sustainable development that helps communities become self-sufficient.
