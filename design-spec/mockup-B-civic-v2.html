Education in Haiti — and how you can sponsor a classroom in Pelerin

Most Haitian schools charge tuition. We make sure cost is never the reason a child stays home.

What is education like in Haiti?

Roughly nine in ten schools in Haiti are privately run and charge tuition, so families pay for education most cannot afford. Adult literacy sits near 61%. In Pelerin, our partner classrooms provide daily meals and supplies so cost stops deciding who learns.

Why is school so hard to afford in Haiti?

Because in Haiti, public school is the exception. Around 90% of schools are privately operated — by churches, communities, and nonprofits — and nearly all charge tuition, plus uniforms, books, and exam fees. Haiti ranks near the bottom of the world in national education spending, so families carry the cost themselves.

For a family earning a few dollars a day, one child's school year can consume a month's income or more. When money runs out, children simply stop attending. Only about one in five eligible children is enrolled in secondary school.

Sources: UNESCO / World Factbook; UNICEF enrollment data.

What's happening in Pelerin?

Pelerin is a hillside community in the Pétionville area above Port-au-Prince — and it's where our last mission trip worked directly with local classrooms. We fund school meals and supplies through people we know by name, and we go back.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to sponsor a child in Haiti?

Traditional matched-child programs charge $30–$43 per month. Our model is different: any amount supports the whole classroom in Pelerin, starting at $5 — enough to feed 25 students for a school day.

Is school free in Haiti?

Mostly, no. Around nine in ten Haitian schools are privately run and charge tuition, plus uniforms and books. Cost is the main reason children in Haiti leave school early.

How much of my donation actually reaches Haiti?

100%. Our payment platform, Zeffy, is funded by optional donor tips — which you can set to $0 — so processing fees never come out of your gift.

Can I give once instead of monthly?

Yes. One-time gifts of any size go straight to meals and supplies. Monthly gifts matter most because they let the school plan ahead.
