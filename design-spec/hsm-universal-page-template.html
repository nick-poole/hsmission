3 · The universal page skeleton

Fixed spine, per-page middle. Every page is this stack with sections toggled — nothing reordered, nothing invented per page.

Nav (navy) + skip link
HERO — image + slab (breadcrumb → eyebrow → H1 → dek → meta)
NAVY — answer block + gold stat tiles
WHITE — article body (TOC · figures · tables · timeline · steps · charts · map)
RED — the one pivotal CTA (omit on utility pages)
WHITE — FAQ (details/summary + FAQPage schema)
GOLD — donate band + Zeffy + trust chips
WHITE — related article cards (3)
BLACK — footer + legal/EIN

Utility pages (contact, legal, thank-you) run: nav → short hero → white → footer. Thank-you is noindex and fires the GA4 conversion.

Hard rules

  • H1 lives in the slab, nothing above it in the DOM — breadcrumbs and eyebrow are nav/paragraph elements, so heading order stays clean.
  • Breadcrumb depth caps at 3 (Home › Section › Page). Trail matches the URL path, and the JSON-LD matches the trail — never more, never fewer.
  • One hero image per page, art-directed right — subject in the right two-thirds, no text baked into images, no stock.
  • Same slab, three heights — 64vh home, 52vh article, 34vh utility. No bespoke heroes.
  • Every article hero shows "Last updated" — matching Article JSON-LD dateModified.
  • Hero is the LCP: preload it, WebP ≤110KB, explicit width/height, 50% opacity over navy so contrast is guaranteed regardless of photo.
