01 · Color & contrast

The palette is the flag family: your gold sits inside the range used for the coat-of-arms golds; navy and red run deeper than the flag's bands for AA text contrast. Gold is never text on white — fills and rules only.

  • --navy #002e5astructure, bands, headings
  • --gold #f3b419tiles, CTAs, rules
  • --red #C1272DCTA band, kickers, accents
  • --black #0c0e11footer only
  • --ink #14273cbody text
  • --gold-ink #3a2e00text on gold
  • --navy-050 #eef3f8washes, callouts
  • --red-tint #ffe3e4quote & alert wash
PairRatioUse

White / navy · mist / navy12:1 · 7:1 ✓Navy bands

02 · Typography

Libre Franklin 700/800/900 for display, Source Sans 3 400/600/700 for body. Fluid clamp() scale, 66ch measure. Self-host, subset, font-display: swap.

H2 Franklin 800 — section questions

Meta / captions / sources — step--1, soft ink.

06 · Section bands — the rhythm

Order is fixed sitewide: navy carries the answer + proof, white carries the article, red carries ONE pivotal ask, gold carries the form, white carries related reading, black closes. Red band is clipped on a 6% diagonal.

NAVY — answer block + gold stat tiles
WHITE — article, tables, figures, FAQ
RED — the pivotal CTA (one per page)
GOLD — donate band with Zeffy
WHITE — related article cards
BLACK — footer

07 · Answer blocks (BLUF)

On navy (page-opening answer)

What is education like in Haiti?Roughly nine in ten schools in Haiti are privately run and charge tuition. Adult literacy sits near 61%. In Pelerin, our partner classrooms provide daily meals and supplies so cost stops deciding who learns.

How much does it cost to feed a child in Haiti?In our Pelerin program, about $0.20 per meal — $5 covers 25 students for a school day.
