About Haitian Sensation Mission

A 501(c)(3) Haiti Nonprofit Organization

Last updated August 2026

Who We Are

Haitian Sensation Mission is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit helping Haiti through direct community action. We focus on what matters most: clean water access, quality education, and disaster recovery. Every initiative we undertake is designed to create real, measurable impact in Haitian communities.

Our Mission

Our mission is to inspire and enable the Haitian population to develop a productive future, and to help protect their basic human rights on their journey toward making a positive difference in their country's destiny.

Our mission has adopted a village in Pelerin, Haiti. With your help, we can make a difference in this small village of approximately 2,000 residents. We would like to focus on building a learning center where the people of Pelerin can learn a trade to help support themselves and their families in the future. Your support will help us give a hand up instead of a hand out.

From Our Founder

“As a small boy in Haiti, I was inspired by watching my mother care for and cook for homeless people in front of a Catholic church. From that point on, I've had a passion in my heart to help others, especially the people of Haiti.”

— Joseph Altenor, Founder

Joseph also owns the Haitian Sensation restaurant and coffee shop, whose story is woven into the mission's own. Read the full story behind the mission.
